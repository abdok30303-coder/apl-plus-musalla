# Wallpaper sources and optimisation

- Wallpapers 01–20: supplied by the package owner through the linked Google Drive folder.
- Wallpaper 21: AI-generated cinematic interpretation of Al Masjid Al Haram.
- Wallpaper 22: AI-generated cinematic interpretation of the Al Aqsa compound.
- Wallpaper 23: AI-generated cinematic interpretation of Al Masjid An Nabawi.

Every wallpaper is centre-cropped to the launcher's 16:9 framing, resized to **1920 × 1080** and encoded as lossy **WebP** (quality 65 for the supplied set; quality 70 for the three mosque artworks). Metadata has been removed. The user-facing Wallpaper setting has precedence over individual app artwork, so manual and timed changes are always visible.

WebP is supported by the Chromium M85 engine on Samsung Tizen 6.5 TVs.
